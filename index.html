Frequently Asked Questions

Before you order.

Can I order a custom size?

Yes. Share your required height, width, reference image and quantity on WhatsApp for a custom quotation.

Are the prices shown final?

The website uses enquiry-based pricing because marble, size, carving, finish and quantity can change the final quote.

How do I place an order?

Add products to the cart, enter your name and city, then click “Order on WhatsApp”. Your selected items will be sent automatically to +91 98297 35516.

Do you make marble temple and architectural pieces?

Yes. The collection includes temple models, mandir panels, jali, chowkis, fountains, decorative pieces and custom marble work.

Can you arrange delivery?

Delivery and packing can be discussed according to product size, quantity and destination.
